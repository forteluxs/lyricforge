## Indonesia Chart — Key Patterns

### Structural norms:
- Intro concept/hook → Verse (narrative) → Pre-Chorus (tension) → Reff (emotional peak) → Bridge (resolution/twist) → Outro
- Many songs have "delayed reveal" twists in bridge (e.g., "rumah baru" = grave in Sesi Potret)

### Dominant lyrical devices:
- **Metaphor of place** (Nadhif, Perunggu): city as love, Bandung/Gambir/kota
- **Religious/spiritual frame** (Raim Laode, Sal Priadi): prayer, senja, magrib, ikhlas
- **Self-aware meta-commentary** (Raim Laode): acknowledging indie clichés while using them
- **Parental love** (.Feast): father to daughter, sacrifice, leaving for work
- **Cosmic scale** (Iqro'): atom, stars, universe as humility device
- **Delayed reveal** (Sesi Potret): narrative twist at emotional climax

### Rhyme pattern:
- Loose assonance, natural speech rhythm
- Repeated reff lines for sing-along
- Prose-like verses, structured refrain

### Key vocabulary pool (poetic Indonesian):
- derita, selimuti, muara, berkecukupan, ikhlas, senja, magrib, pelangi, doa, abadi
- Contrast: formal/poetic (tatkala, ufuk) vs casual (udah, gak)
