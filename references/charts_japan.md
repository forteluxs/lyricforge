## Japan Chart — Structural & Stylistic Patterns

### Structure:
- Very common: **Intro → V1 → Pre-C → C → V2 → Pre-C → C → Bridge/Interlude → C (climax) → Outro**
- Anime themes often build from quiet Intro to explosive C

### Lyrical devices:
- **Paradox/contradiction** (lulu., AIZO): warmth of painful memories, love-hate
- **Body/physical imagery** (IRIS OUT): carotid artery, blood, physical sensation
- **Nature metaphors** (lulu.): planet, descendants, celestial
- **Abstract concepts made physical** (King Gnu): truth/lies as tangible forces
- **Repetition for intensity** (IRIS OUT): "darling darling", "love me love me hate me"

### Key themes:
- Obsessive love (Kenshi Yonezu, King Gnu)
- Bittersweet endings and memory (Mrs. GREEN APPLE)
- Identity and youth (Number_i, M!LK)
- Violence/chaos as metaphor for emotion (Chainsaw Man, Jujutsu Kaisen themes)

### Rhyme:
- Japanese uses assonance more than strict rhyme
- English lyrics mixed in (IRIS OUT, AIZO) for emotional punch
- Katakana English words used for rhythm (e.g., "Avada Kedavra", "iris out")
