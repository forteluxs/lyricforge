## Germany + France — Key Patterns

### Germany:
- Turkish-German artists represent a major mainstream force (not niche)
- K-pop crossovers (ROSÉ, BLACKPINK) have strong German audiences
- International English-language collabs dominate #1 spots

### France:
- Afrobeats/Afro-French pop is THE dominant sound (Aya Nakamura blueprint)
- French rap uses: verlan, African slang, street vocabulary
- Literary references in rap titles (Pocahontas, Melodrama, René Caovilla)
- Latin music has strong crossover (Bad Bunny charting naturally)

### Structural notes:
- French rap: Hook-heavy, verses in flowing French, minimal bridge
- Afrobeats: Groove-based, call-response in chorus, percussion-forward
- Turkish-German pop: Emotional chorus (Turkish tradition) + rap verses (German/Turkish)
